The Anchor Yield Reserve maintains complete insulation from principal volatility, providing algorithmic capital protection.

The Foundation reserves the right to amend terms and modify yield rates at its sole discretion without prior notice to depositors. Rate parameters and borrow incentives may be unilaterally altered based upon internal governance consensus.
