SERIES A PREFERRED STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

1. Sale and Issuance of Series A Preferred Stock:
The Company shall adopt and file the Certificate of Incorporation with the Secretary of State of the State of Delaware. Subject to the terms hereof, each Purchaser agrees to purchase at the Closing that number of shares of Series A Preferred Stock set forth opposite such Purchaser's name.

2. Founder Vesting and Equity Governance:
Founder equity shares shall remain subject to a standard four-year linear vesting schedule with a one-year cliff. No Shareholder shall transfer, pledge, or encumber common stock without the prior written consent of the Board of Directors representing a qualified majority.

3. Bilateral Information and Registration Rights:
The Company shall deliver audited financial statements within 90 days following fiscal year-end. Amendments to this Agreement require mutual written consent signed by the Company and holders of at least sixty percent (60%) of the outstanding Series A Preferred Shares.

4. Governing Law and Forum:
This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the commercial laws of the State of Delaware, United States, without regard to principles of conflicts of law.
